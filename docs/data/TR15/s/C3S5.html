TRICARE Reimbursement Manual 6010.61-M, April 1, 2015
Operational Requirements
Chapter 3
Section 5
Reimbursement Administration
Issue Date:  
Authority:  
Revision:  
The instructions contained in this chapter are to be followed in reimbursing all TRICARE claims provided by non-network providers. In those cases in which a contractor is uncertain how to apply a procedure or where a contractor encounters a situation which is not covered in this chapter, the contractor shall request guidance from the Contracting Officer (CO) or Contracting Officer’s Representative (COR).
