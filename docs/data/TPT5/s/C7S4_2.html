2.0  POLICY
Modified in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: 2.1  End Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) services, hemodialysis,Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: peritoneal dialysis, treatment for Acute Kidney Injury (AKI), and other dialysis services and supplies are covered.Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: Covered services include:
Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: •  Inpatient dialysis treatments when medically necessary and appropriate.
Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: •  Outpatient dialysis treatments and outpatient individual professional provider services rendered by TRICARE-authorized providers.
Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: •  Home dialysis, including home dialysis training and home dialysis equipment and supplies. Note: Dialysis training must be medically necessary and documented in the medical record, for example, if additional training is required because of a change in equipment.
Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: •  Other drugs, services, and supplies that are medically necessary and appropriate, and required as part of the dialysis care (e.g., laboratory services).
Modified in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: 2.2  Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: The contractor shall ensure that the dialysis course of treatment is medically necessary and appropriate, and that the attending or prescribing individual professional provider has documented the frequency of dialysis services in the patient’s medical record and plan of care.
Modified in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: 3.0  Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: EXCLUSION
Added in Revision 5, June 20, 2024: The services of dialysis aids, attendants, or assistants.
