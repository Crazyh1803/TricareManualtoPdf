Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TRICARE Manual Downloader app ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our application. We are committed to being transparent about our practices.

Disclaimer: The App is an independent, unofficial tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Department of Defense, Defense Health Agency, or TRICARE. All manual content displayed in the App originates from the official source at manuals.health.mil, which is linked from within the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects minimal information necessary to function:

  • App settings and preferences — such as your selected manuals and display options. These are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers beyond what Android provides to all apps by default
  • Usage analytics or crash reporting data sent to us

2. How the App Works

The App downloads publicly available TRICARE manuals directly from official U.S. Department of Defense / Defense Health Agency websites (manuals.health.mil). These are public government documents. The App acts as a convenience tool to retrieve and organize this content on your device. We do not host or modify the source documents.

3. Data Storage

All data used by the App — including downloaded manuals and your settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive or store your data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App downloads content from manuals.health.mil, an official U.S. government website operated by the Defense Health Agency. Your device's connection to that site is subject to the DHA's own privacy practices. We do not share your information with any other third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
