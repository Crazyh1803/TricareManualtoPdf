TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
Demonstrations, Pilot Projects, and Value-Based Initiatives
Chapter 18
Section 13
Virtual Value Networks (VVNs) Pilot
Revision:  C-66, September 18, 2026
1.0  PURPOSE
1.1  The purpose of the VVN Pilot is to meet requirements set forth in the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2017 (NDAA FY 2017), Sections 701(h) and 705. Section 701(h) direct a pilot program to demonstrate and assess the feasibility of incorporating value-based health care methodologies in the Private Sector component of the TRICARE program through the use of high-value providers. Section 705(a) authorizes the implementation of value-based incentive programs to encourage health care providers under the TRICARE program (including physicians, hospitals, and other persons and facilities involved in providing such health care services) to improve the following:
1.1.1  The quality of health care provided to covered beneficiaries under the TRICARE program;
1.1.2  The experience of covered beneficiaries in receiving health care under the TRICARE program; and
1.1.3  The health of covered beneficiaries.
1.2  The VVN Pilot is intended to measure, identify, and designate high-value preferred providers, defined as those demonstrating high quality and low-waste, and evaluate approaches to encourage beneficiary utilization of these providers through targeted program offerings and enhancements. The VVN Pilot proposes a series of enhancements aimed to support beneficiary choice and create cost and quality efficiencies by encouraging the use of high-value providers.
1.3  The VVN Pilot will support the Quadruple Aim by:
•  Providing beneficiaries with more information on provider quality and performance within the Pilot region purchased care network to support informed selection of providers and improve care outcomes and experiences.
•  Provide participating providers with increased performance information and feedback to support improvements in cost control and quality outcomes.
•  Assess whether directing care towards preferred providers results in measurable improvement and confidence in clinical outcomes, beneficiary satisfaction, and resource utilization.
2.0  BACKGROUND
2.1  VBSM is an emerging strategy in the health care industry that utilizes a structured approach for guiding health services delivery so that it is aligned with patient-relevant outcomes and cost-effectiveness rather than simply maximizing the numbers of services provided. The intent is to enhance the beneficiary’s experience of care and reduce health care costs over time. The framework guides patients or members toward high-value providers and services- those that deliver the best outcomes at the most reasonable cost.
2.2  The T-5 core VBSM acts as the foundation of the T-5 VVN Pilot, allowing the contractor to create differentiated and enhanced tools to drive meaningful attention to preferred providers and support choice efficacy for TRICARE Select beneficiaries.
2.3  The T-5 VVN will implement programs to increase utilization of preferred providers and monitor effectiveness of interventions. Using the VBSM as a backbone, the VVN program tools and features deployed throughout the life of the Pilot will enhance TRICARE Select beneficiary engagement with preferred providers.
2.4  Phase I of the pilot will be the pre-implementation gathering of the initial year’s data to set a baseline by which to establish desired future metric goals. Phase II would be reporting and observing data and determining if adjustments, advertisement, provider changes, etc. need to be modified. Phase III would be the final data reporting to determine the value of this pilot and provide recommendations.
3.0  APPLICABILITY
3.1  The VVN Pilot is applicable to designated ZIP codes in the geographic areas designated by the contractor and DHA. The VVN Pilot’s period of performance is within the VVN Pilot contract.
3.2  The VVN Pilot primarily targets, but is not limited to, TRICARE Select beneficiaries. This differs from the VBSM, which primarily targets the TRICARE Prime beneficiary population.
4.0  OBJECTIVES
4.1  The objectives of the VVN Pilot are to support evaluation of whether providing beneficiaries with access to standardized provider value information and targeted engagement tools influences provider selection behavior and results in improved quality, and more efficient use of health care resources.
4.2  To support this evaluation, the pilot will implement a beneficiary-facing guide that supports the selection of preferred providers through the use of standardized, validated, third-party metrics addressing appropriateness of care, clinical outcomes, and cost.
4.3  The specific objectives of the pilot across two phases are to:
4.3.1  Improve the quality of health care delivered, the overall health of covered beneficiaries, and the experience of selecting providers for TRICARE Select beneficiaries in the Pilot Regions.
4.3.2  Encourage beneficiaries to choose preferred providers by offering experience enhancements and features including tailored communication, deeper visibility to cost and quality metrics, clinical support, and advanced analytics suggestion technology.
4.3.3  Support cost savings and enhanced quality care delivery for TRICARE beneficiaries by enhancing beneficiaries’ understanding of preferred provider options.
4.3.4  Deliver enhanced and integrated technology solutions across the beneficiary experience continuum related to information communication, education, provider selection, and clinical support.
4.3.5  Collect sufficient data to determine whether the VVN Pilot initiative results in a measurable increase in the use of high-value providers and if the usage of the high-value providers have improved the patient satisfaction, overall health outcomes, and reduction in unnecessary costs. This VVN Pilot initiative’s goal is to provide a positive measurable increase in order to support integration and usage in future managed care support contracts.
5.0  POLICY
5.1  Eligibility
5.1.1  TRICARE Select beneficiaries in the Pilot areas across the East region will have access to the provider value rating tools but only TRICARE Select beneficiaries in the chosen markets will be eligible for program measurement. Similarly, only TRICARE Select beneficiaries in the selected markets will be eligible to receive enhanced communications and clinical interventions.
5.1.2  TRICARE Prime beneficiaries residing in the VVN designated service area may voluntarily elect to utilize the VVN identified providers.
5.2  Benefit
The VVN Pilot will match the TRICARE Select benefit plan, consistent with the contract year offerings.
5.3  Customer Service and Education
5.3.1  The contractor shall provide education to TRICARE Select beneficiaries in the VVN-designated areas about the VVN Pilot with oversight and assistance from DHA. The contractor will submit the education plan outlining how it intends to educate the beneficiaries and providers on the VVN Pilot program. The Government will review the plan and provide appropriate feedback for recommended changes.
5.3.2  The contractor shall provide education rates from outreach (ex number of beneficiaries reached, communication types, survey data) to the Government on a quarterly and annual basis.
6.0  PROGRAM OVERVIEW
6.1  From the VVN Pilot’s enrollee perspective, this will be a TRICARE Select option. TRICARE Prime beneficiaries will not be included in outreach efforts though they may be able to see high value provider indicators through the Provider Search Tool (PST).
6.2  The pilot will focus on tracking and providing high value provider information for the following specialties chosen for the purposes of utilization volume and clinical simplicity:
6.2.1  Select Population Visiting Primary Care Manager (PCM)
6.2.2  Select Population Visiting Cardiologist
6.2.3  Select Population Visiting Endocrinologist
6.2.4  Select Population Visiting Gastroenterologist
6.3  The VVN Pilot utilizes a suite of contractor developed capabilities and services working in concert to drive beneficiaries to choose high-value providers. High-value providers are determined based on historical cost control and clinical quality compliance.
6.4  The pilot is intended to have two phases spanning over three years, in which the Defense Health Agency (DHA) intends to gather contractor data which will establish a baseline used to develop goals for evaluative metrics to be implemented for the next two years.
6.5  The DHA will determine whether to recommend integrating VVN into future managed care support contracts after the VVN Pilot’s termination.
6.6  Phase I – Baseline Metric Collection
6.6.1  Upon commencement of the VVN Pilot the contractor shall obtain the commercial metric data for the identification of the high-value providers to be noted in the PST. The contractor shall then gather the baseline data and provide the report on the utilization of the PST, the utilization of the high-value network providers, and the cost metrics associated with the high-value provider usage. These metrics will generate the baseline for determining the VVN Pilot improvement targets.
6.6.2  The contractor shall make the following enhancements to existing processes, platforms, and technology:
6.6.2.1  Enhanced Communication Interventions
6.6.2.1.1  The contractor shall use dynamic materials and create mature high-touch, disease-specific tailored experiences based on beneficiary communication preferences to increase engagement and buy-in to encourage beneficiaries to choose high-value providers.
6.6.2.1.2  The contractors email communications to beneficiaries will be measured according to paragraph 8.0.
6.6.2.1.3  The contractor shall refine processes and communication modalities leveraged to support VVN Pilot efforts over the life of the pilot as communication enhancements are expected to mature.
6.6.2.2  Clinical Fast-Pass
6.6.2.2.1  The contractor shall create a channel for beneficiaries to immediately opt-in for clinical support based on selection of preferred provider and specific condition to facilitate deeper whole-health clinical support for beneficiaries with endocrinology, gastroenterology, and cardiology or PCM related conditions.
6.6.2.2.2  The contractor shall leverage its current Disease Management (DM) programming to provide high-touch care to eligible TRICARE Select beneficiaries who opt-in.
6.6.2.2.3  The contractor shall ensure their programming follows DM procedures and protocols, but shall not measure this programming with their other contract requirements related to DM.
6.6.2.3  Non-TRICARE Data Enhancement
6.6.2.3.1  The contractor shall include medical and commercial data, if applicable, into provider rating to create discernible cost and quality metrics on nearly all contracted primary and clinical specialties.
6.6.2.3.2  The contractor shall modify the PST to ensure clear, legible, and accessible badges, icons, and other information is available to beneficiaries, highlighting the top two providers in their area by specialty.
6.6.2.4  Provider Recommendation Tool (PRT)
6.6.2.4.1  The contractor shall utilize a beneficiary facing provider recommendation service (e.g., “people like you chose…”) to increases decision making by eliminating unlikely options and providing immediate data-driven guidance which informs beneficiaries on the highest value options for their care.
6.6.2.4.2  The contractor shall design a machine learning model to interface with PST for seamless integration of the PRT.

7.0  GOVERNMENT RESPONSIBILITY
The Government will perform periodic reviews and evaluations of the pilot per Contract Data Requirements List (CDRL) report requirements monthly, quarterly, and annually. The Government will work with the contractor to establish the target metrics in Phase II of the VVN Pilot after the data baseline for each service line has been established in Phase I.
8.0  CONTRACTOR RESPONSIBILITIES
8.1  The contractor shall provide reports as described in the CDRL, DD Form 1423. Details for reporting are identified in DD Form 1423, CDRL, located in Section J of the applicable contract.
8.2  The contractor shall provide education to beneficiaries and providers on the pilot at time of program start, when new eligible beneficiaries enter the pilot region, or at the direction of the Government.
8.3  The contractor shall manage and resolve all inquiries related to the pilot from the beneficiaries through an established call center line for both providers and beneficiaries.
8.4  The contractor shall report trends for questions, complaints, and inquiries in accordance with the quarterly and annual CDRLs.
8.5  The contractor shall make provider value rating tools and metrics available to TRICARE East beneficiaries in the selected pilot regions on a continuous 24/7 basis through secure Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)-compliant, technology platforms to enable more informed beneficiary health care decision making and access to high quality care.
9.0  EXCLUSIONS
Services provided to a beneficiary not residing in a pilot region.
10.0  REPORTS
The contractor shall submit reports in accordance with the CDRL, DD Form 1423, as required by Section J of the applicable contract.
11.0  EFFECTIVE AND TERMINATION DATES
The VVN Pilot shall begin when the contract start date is determined and will terminate no later than (NLT) three years from the start date, subject to the Government’s discretion under Section 1, paragraph 4.2.
