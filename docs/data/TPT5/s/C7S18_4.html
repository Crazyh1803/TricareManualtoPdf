2.0  DESCRIPTION
Treatment of ailments by manipulation to detect and correct faulty structure based in part on the theory that the human body is capable of making its own remedies if its physical and physiological integrity is intact.
3.0  POLICY
Benefits for osteopathic manipulative therapy are covered.
