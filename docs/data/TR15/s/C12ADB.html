Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Timing:Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Each 30-day period is identified as either Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: EarlyAdded in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: or Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: LateAdded in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: . The first 30-day period is classified as early. All subsequent 30-day periods in the sequence (second or later) are classified as late.
Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Admission Source:Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Each 30-day period is identified by the admission source (institutional or community) depending on what healthcare setting was utilized in the 14 days prior to home health admission. Late 30-day periods are always classified as a community admission unless there was an acute hospitalization in the 14 days prior to the late home health 30-day period. A post-acute stay in the 14 days prior to a late home health 30-day period would not be classified as an institutional admission unless the patient had been discharged from home health prior to post-acute stay.
Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Clinical Grouping:Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Each 30-day period is grouped into one of 12 clinical groups based on the patient’s principal diagnosis on the claim.
Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Functional Impairment Level:Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Each period is identified as having either low, medium, or high functional impairment based on the scores for eight OASIS items.
Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: Comorbidity Adjustment:Added in Revision 46, March 26, 2020: A low, high, or no comorbidity adjustment may be made based on the presence of secondary diagnoses on the claim.
