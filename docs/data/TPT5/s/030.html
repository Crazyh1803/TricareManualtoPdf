Administration
Chapter 2
Section 3.2
Neonatal And Pediatric Critical Care Services
Issue Date:  October 23, 2003
Authority:  32 CFR 199.4(b) and (c)
Copyright:  CPT only © 2006 American Medical Association (or such other date of publication of CPT). All Rights Reserved.
Revision:  
1.0  CPT CODES
99293 - 99296, 99298, 99299
2.0  DESCRIPTION
Services provided by a physician directing the care of a critically ill neonate/infant.
3.0  POLICY
3.1  Neonatal and pediatric critical care services may be cost-shared.
3.2  Hospital-grade electric breast pumps may be covered for premature infants under certain circumstances (see Chapter 8, Section 2.6).
