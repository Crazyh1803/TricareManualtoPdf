TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 17
Supplemental Health Care Program (SHCP)
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 17 regarding the SHCP do not apply to the TMEP contractor unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
