Section 17
Supplemental Health Care Program (SHCP)
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 17 regarding the SHCP shall not apply to the TMEPAdded in Revision 12, August 14, 2024: contractor unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
