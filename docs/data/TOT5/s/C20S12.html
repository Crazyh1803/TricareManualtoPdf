TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 12
Appeals And Hearings
Revision:  
All TRICARE requirements Chapter 12 regarding appeals and hearings shall apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  General
Appeals of Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF) preauthorizations follow concurrent review procedures.
