TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.59-M, April 1, 2015
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 21
TRICARE Alaska
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
The specific requirements noted in Chapter 21 regarding TRICARE Alaska do not apply. The TMEP shall follow the TRICARE requirements outlined in the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
