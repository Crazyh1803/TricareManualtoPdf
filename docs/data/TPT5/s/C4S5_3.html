2.0  DESCRIPTION
Prophylactic mastectomy, prophylactic oophorectomy, and prophylactic hysterectomy are surgical procedures that aim at completely removing organs or tissue in the absence of malignant disease to reduce the risk of individuals at high risk from developing cancer.Added in Revision 46, March 10, 2026: These surgeries are also called risk-reducing surgeries.
