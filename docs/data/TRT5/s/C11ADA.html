Hospice Care Rates
Revision:  C-16, January 8, 2025
1.0  FY 2022
The payment rates were increased by the market basket update of 2.0%.
Effective January 1, 2016, two separate payment rates will be applicable for Routine Home Care (RHC). A higher RHC rate for days 1 through 60, and a lower RHC rate for days 61 and beyond of a hospice episode of care, will replace the single rate.
The following national hospice rates are for care and services provided on or after October 1, 2021, through September 30, 2022. The hospice rates applicable to the above period are:
