TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.59-M, April 1, 2015
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 19
Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements regarding the HIPAA, Chapter 19, unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
- END -
