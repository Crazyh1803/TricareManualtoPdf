Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: All TRICARE requirements in Chapter 5Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: regarding network development do not apply to TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: Provider Networks
Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The TMEP contractor shall comply with the requirements in Chapter 5, Section 1, paragraphs 3.4 and 3.5 for services provided to a TRICARE/Medicare dual eligible beneficiary by a network provider when TRICARE is primary payer.
