TRICARE Policy Manual 6010.63-M, April 2021
Administration
Chapter 1
Section 5.2
Medical Photography
Issue Date:  January 23, 1984
Authority:  32 CFR 199.4(g)(74)
Revision:  
1.0  Policy
Photographs, slides, movies or video tapings, including the services of a medical photographer, are not covered even when required to make a benefit determination. Regardless of the indication, whole body integumentary photography is not covered.
