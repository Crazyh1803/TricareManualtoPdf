TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 10
Claims Adjustments and Recoupments
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All The TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 10 regarding claims adjustments and recoupments shall apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section (as indicated below), the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  General
The TMEP contractor shall comply with the provisions of Chapter 10 are applicable to TMEP with the exception of Section 2, the provisions for financially underwritten funds.
