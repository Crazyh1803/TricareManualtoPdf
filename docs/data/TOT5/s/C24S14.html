1.0  General
All TRICARE requirements regarding audits, inspections, reports, and plans shall apply to the TOP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by the provisions of this section; the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), Chapter 12; or the TRICARE contract for health care support services outside the 50 United States (US) and the District of Columbia (hereinafter referred to as the “TOP contract”). See Chapter 14 for additional instructions.
2.0  AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS
2.1  The TRICARE Overseas Currency reports, and the claims supporting them, are subject to audit by Defense Health Agency (DHA) or other authorized Government auditors as part of any financial audit.
2.2  The claims auditing software requirements outlined in the TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), Chapter 1, Section 3 do not apply to TOP claims.
