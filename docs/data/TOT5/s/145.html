TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 23
TRICARE Pharmacy (TPharm)
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 23 regarding pharmacy shall do not apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
