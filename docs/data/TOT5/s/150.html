TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 28
Prescription Monitoring Program (PMP)
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements as specified in Chapter 28 regarding the PMP shall apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
The TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements in Chapter 28 regarding the PMP as specified in Chapter 28.
