TRICARE Reimbursement Manual 6010.64-M, April 2021
Skilled Nursing Facilities (SNFs)
Chapter 8
Addendum H
Wage Indexes For Rural Areas For Skilled Nursing Facilities (SNFs) Based On Core-Based Statistical Area (CBSA) Labor Market Areas
Revision:  
1.0  FISCAL YEAR 2018 FOR SERVICES RECEIVED PRIOR TO OCTOBER 1, 2018
Source: 82 FR 36539. Medicare Program; Prospective Payment System and Consolidated Billing for SNFs for FY 2018.
The Wage Indexes for Rural Areas for SNFs based on CBSA Labor Market Areas applicable to FY 2018 has been combined into one document for rural and urban areas as set forth in Tables available on the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) website at http://www.cms.gov/Medicare/Medicare-Fee-for-Service-Payment/SNFPPS/WageIndex.html.
2.0  FISCAL YEAR 2019 FOR SERVICES RECEIVED PRIOR TO OCTOBER 1, 2019
Source: 83 FR 39162. Medicare Program; Prospective Payment System and Consolidated Billing for SNFs for FY 2019.
The Wage Indexes for Rural Areas for SNFs based on CBSA Labor Market Areas applicable to FY 2019 has been combined into one document for rural and urban areas as set forth in Tables available on the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) website at http://www.cms.gov/Medicare/Medicare-Fee-for-Service-Payment/SNFPPS/WageIndex.html.
