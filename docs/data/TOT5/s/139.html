TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 17
Supplemental Health Care Program (SHCP)
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 17 regarding the SHCP shall not apply to the TMEP contractor unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
