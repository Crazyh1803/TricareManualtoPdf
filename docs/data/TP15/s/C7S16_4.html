Section 16.4
Education And Training For Patient Self Management

Revision:  
1.0  CPT PROCEDURE CODES
98960 - 98962
2.0  DESCRIPTION
Education and training services to teach the patient (may include care giver) how to effectively self manage the patient’s illness(es)/disease(s) or delay disease comorbidity(ies) in conjunction with the patient’s professional health care team.
3.0  POLICY
Self help services, except for Diabetes Self-Management Training (DSMT), are excluded from coverage by regulation. DSMT is covered (see Chapter 8, Section 8.1).
