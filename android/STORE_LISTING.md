# Play Store listing — TRICARE Manuals

Text to paste into Play Console. Written to answer the Misleading Claims
violation: the listing and the app must state plainly that this is an
unofficial app, and must point to the official government source.

The two things the review asked for are both in the app itself as well as
here — a disclaimer on the first screen (not buried in Settings, which is
where the only previous one lived) and links to manuals.dha.mil from the
reader and from Settings > About.

---

## App name (30 chars max)

```
TRICARE Manuals
```

Keeping the existing name — decided by the owner. The unofficial status is
carried by the short description, the full description, and the in-app
disclaimer on the first screen rather than by the name itself.

## Short description (80 chars max)

```
Unofficial offline reader for the public TRICARE manuals. Not a government app.
```

## Full description

```
An unofficial, independently developed reader for the TRICARE manuals.

IMPORTANT — THIS IS NOT A GOVERNMENT APP
This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or maintained by the U.S.
Government, the Department of Defense, the Defense Health Agency, or TRICARE.
It is an independent tool built to make publicly available documents easier
to read offline.

OFFICIAL SOURCE
All manual content is reproduced from the official publications at
https://manuals.dha.mil — the Defense Health Agency's TRICARE Manuals site.
Every section in the app links back to its official page, and the official
site is linked from Settings > About.

Content is copied periodically and may lag behind the official publication.
Always confirm against the official source before relying on it for
eligibility, coverage, reimbursement, or any other decision.

WHAT THE APP DOES
• Read the TRICARE Policy, Reimbursement, Operations and Systems manuals,
  and 32 CFR Part 199, offline
• Download a manual as Markdown or PDF
• Bookmark and highlight sections
• Search within a manual
• Dark theme, adjustable text size

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
• It does not provide medical, legal, or benefits advice
• It does not connect to any TRICARE account or handle personal health data
• It does not collect personal information — see the privacy policy

The manuals themselves are public U.S. Government works. This app adds no
official status to them.
```

## Data safety

- No personal or sensitive user data is collected.
- No data is shared with third parties.
- Network access is used only to download public manual content.
