TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 15
Markets/Military Medical Treatment Facilities (MTF) and Contractor Interfaces
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 15 regarding Markets/MTFs and contractor Interfaces shall not apply to the TMEP contractor unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
