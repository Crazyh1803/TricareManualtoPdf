# Play Store listing text (Google Play Console)

This text is not read by the app or CI — the Play Console listing is edited
by hand. This file exists so the copy is tracked and easy to paste in.

Written to resolve a Play Console "Misleading Claims" policy flag:
*Missing Source Link for Government Information*. The fix requires the
store listing to (1) name the official government source with a working
URL, and (2) carry an easy-to-see disclaimer that the app is not a
government entity. See the "How to fix" steps in the Play Console warning
for background.

## Full description (en-US)

Stop scrolling through endless PDFs. Start getting answers.

TRICARE manuals are essential, but reading them on the official website is
a challenge. UnLock Tricare is the bridge between complex government
documentation and the power of modern AI. Our app simplifies the process
of accessing, downloading, and preparing TRICARE manuals so you can upload
them to your favorite AI assistant for instant analysis.

**Official source:** All manual content in this app is sourced directly
from the U.S. Defense Health Agency's official TRICARE Manuals site,
https://manuals.health.mil. Every manual in the app links back to its
official page on that site.

**Disclaimer:** UnLock Tricare is an independent, unofficial tool and is
not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Department of
Defense, the Defense Health Agency, or TRICARE.

Why UnLock Tricare?
Official manuals are often buried in difficult-to-navigate web interfaces
or formatted in ways that make deep reading impossible. UnLock Tricare
does the heavy lifting for you, allowing you to "unlock" the data within
these documents — while always keeping a clear link back to the official
source at manuals.health.mil.

Key Features:
- Direct Access: Quickly browse and locate the latest TRICARE Policy,
  Benefit, and Reimbursement manuals, each linked to its official source
  on manuals.health.mil.
- One-Tap Downloads: Save manuals directly to your device with ease.
- AI-Ready Files: Downloads are optimized for easy upload into AI
  platforms (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini).
- Search Less, Know More: By moving manuals into an AI environment, you
  can ask specific questions about coverage, claims, and policies instead
  of searching line-by-line.

How it Works:
1. Browse: Select the specific manual or chapter you need.
2. Download: Save the file to your mobile device.
3. Analyze: Upload the file to your preferred AI tool to summarize complex
   sections or find specific benefit details in seconds.

Empower your healthcare journey. Whether you are a provider, a service
member, or a family member, UnLock Tricare puts the information you need
right where you can actually use it — always with a clear path back to the
official government source.
