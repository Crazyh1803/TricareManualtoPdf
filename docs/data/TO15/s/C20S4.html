TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 4
Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: Provider Certifications And Credentialing
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements in Chapter 4Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: regarding provider certification unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: General
Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The provisions regarding credentialing, Market/Military Treatment Facility (MTF), clinical support agreements, and network provider requirements are not applicable to TMEP.
- END -
