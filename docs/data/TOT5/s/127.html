TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 5
Provider Networks
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 5 regarding network development shall do not apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section (as indicated below), or stated in the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  Provider Networks
The provisions of TMEP contractor shall comply with all requirements listed in Chapter 5, Section 1, paragraphs 9.03.4 and 10.03.5 apply to TMEP and may be applicable for services provided to a TRICARE/Medicare dual eligible beneficiary by a network provider when TRICARE is primary payer.
