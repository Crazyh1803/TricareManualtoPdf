TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Overseas Program (TOP)
Chapter 24
Section 27
28
Health Insurance Portability And Accountability Act (HIPAA) Of 1996
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
1.0  General
All TRICARE requirements regarding HIPAA of 1996 standards and privacy of Individually Identifiable Health Information (IIHI) shall apply to the TOP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section; the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), Chapter 12; or the TRICARE contract for health care support services outside the 50 United States (US) and the District of Columbia (hereinafter referred to as the “TOP contract”). See Chapter 19 for additional instructions.
