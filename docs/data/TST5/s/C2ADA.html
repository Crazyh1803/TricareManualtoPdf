TRICARE COUNTRY AND/OR ISLAND
CODE3
In accordance with Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) requirements, TRICARE utilizes the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 3166 for country and island code determination. The ISO 3166 can also be used if more detailed information is required to assign territories and islands into these countries.
1  Outside of the Continental United States (OCONUS) Geographic Area of Responsibility: Region 13 = Eurasia-Africa, Region 14 = Pacific, and Region 15 = Latin America.
2  The TRICARE Southeast (Region 3)/Latin America & Canada (Region 15 and Region 22) Government Designated Authority (GDA), TRICARE Regional Offices (TROs) is responsible for health care support for beneficiaries residing in Canada (CA), as well as for beneficiaries residing in Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands.
3  Edits 1-020-01 and 2-020-01 use this table to check validity.
4  OCONUS TRICARE Reserve Select (TRS) Region: Region 20 = Eurasia-Africa, Region 21 = Pacific, and Region 22 = Latin America.
