TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 15
Markets/Military Treatment Facilities (MTFs) And Contractor Interfaces
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 15 shall not apply to the TMEP contractor unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
