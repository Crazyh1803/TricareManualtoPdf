TRICARE Policy Manual 6010.63-M, April 2021
Providers
Chapter 11
Section 2.2
Employer-Operated Medical Facilities
Issue Date:  January 23, 1984
Authority:  32 CFR 199.4(g)(11) and (g)(12)
Revision:  
1.0  ISSUE
Whether benefits may be extended for care provided by employer-operated medical facilities.
2.0  BACKGROUND
Medical facilities operated by employers, labor unions or other organizations for the benefit of their employees ordinarily do not impose charges for their services.
3.0  POLICY
Benefits are provided on the basis of incurred expenses; that is, the beneficiary must actually be billed for a service and have a legal obligation to pay the bill. When no charge is incurred by the patient, TRICARE will not cost-share or apply any amounts to satisfy a deductible or cost-share.
