TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.59-M, April 1, 2015
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 25
TRICARE Young Adult (TYA) Program
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements in Chapter 25 regarding TYA unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  General
1.1  The requirements in Chapter 25, Section 1 specific to enrollment and collection of premiums do not apply.
1.2  The TMEP contractor shall process claims in accordance with TRM, Chapter 4, Section 4. The TMEP contractor shall perform customer service functions to the same extent as other TMEP beneficiaries.
- END -
