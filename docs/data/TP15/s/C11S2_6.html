1.0  ISSUE
Psychiatric hospitals accreditation.
2.0  DESCRIPTION
A psychiatric hospital is an institution which is engaged primarily in providing services to inpatients for the diagnosis and treatment of mental disorders.
3.0  POLICY
In order for the services of a psychiatric hospital to be covered, the hospital shall comply with the provisions outlined in 32 CFR 199.6(b)(4)(i). All Added in Revision 13, November 15, 2017: freestanding psychiatric hospitals shall be Added in Revision 13, November 15, 2017: currently accredited under the Joint Commission’s Added in Revision 13, November 15, 2017: (TJC’s) Accreditation Manual for Hospitals (AMH) standardsAdded in Revision 13, November 15, 2017: , or an accrediting organization approved by the Director, Defense Health Agency (DHA), in order for their services to be cost-shared. In the case of those psychiatric hospitals that are not Joint Commission-accredited because they have not been in operation a sufficient period of time to be eligible to request an accreditation survey by Added in Revision 13, November 15, 2017: TJC, the Director, Added in Revision 13, November 15, 2017: DHA, or a designee, may grant temporary approval if the hospital is certified and participating under Title XVIII of the Social Security Act (Medicare, Part A). This temporary approval expires 12 months from the date on which the psychiatric hospital first becomes eligible to request an accreditation survey by the Joint Commission. For further information on accreditation of psychiatric hospitals refer to 32 CFR 199.6(b)(4)(iv).
