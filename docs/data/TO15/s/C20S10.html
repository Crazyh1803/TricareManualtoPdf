TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.59-M, April 1, 2015
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 10
Claims Adjustments And Recoupments
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 10 regarding claims adjustments and recoupments unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section (as indicated below), the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  General
The requirements pertaining to Financially Underwritten Funds do not apply to the TMEP contractor.
- END -
