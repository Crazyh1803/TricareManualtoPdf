Providers
Chapter 11
Section 11.1
Birthing Center Accreditation
Issue Date:  May 23, 1988
Authority:  32 CFR 199.6(b)(4)(xi)
Revision:  
1.0  ISSUE
What is acceptable birthing center accreditation?
2.0  POLICY
2.1  Only accreditation by one of the following organizations meets the requirement of 32 CFR 199.6(b)(4)(xi)(A)(3) that the center be accredited by a nationally recognized accreditation organization whose standards and procedures have been determined to be acceptable by the Defense Health Agency (DHA), or a designee:
•  The Joint Commission (TJC).
•  The Accreditation Association for Ambulatory Health Care (AAAHC).
•  The Commission for the Accreditation of Birth Centers (CABC).
2.2  The continued status of an accreditation organization being “acceptable to the DHA, or a designee” is discretionary on the part of the DHA and is, therefore, subject to change.
2.3  Should an organization lose status as “acceptable to the DHA, or a designee”, accreditation granted to a birthing center prior to the accreditation organization’s loss of status is subject to review by DHA to determine whether the birthing center should remain a certified provider for a period not to exceed the date that the existing accreditation would have otherwise expired.
- END -
