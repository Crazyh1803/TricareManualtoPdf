Section 9.1
Electronystagmography (ENG)
Issue Date:  July 8, 1998
Authority:  32 CFR 199.4(c)(2)(xiv)
Copyright:  CPT only © 2006 American Medical Association (or such other date of publication of CPT). All Rights Reserved.
Revision:  C-21, October 11, 2024
1.0  CPT CODE RANGE
92541 - 92547
2.0  DESCRIPTION
ENG refers to the recording of ocular nystagmus or eye movements by electrooculography in response to vestibular dysfunction. During ENG testing, the eye movements are recorded and analyzed by placing small electrodes on the skin around the eyes.
3.0  POLICY
3.1  ENG testing may be cost-shared to determine the diagnosis of vestibular system abnormalities including disorders that affect the peripheral or central vestibular system when ordered by a physician.
3.2  ENG testing should be reserved for the assessment of patients with vertigo, dizziness, or dysequilibrium and who are suspected of suffering from the following vestibular system abnormalities: The list of indications is not all inclusive. Other indications are covered when documented by reliable evidence as safe, effective, and comparable or superior to standard care (proven).
•  Meniere’s disease/Endolymphatic hydrops
•  Vestibular neuritis
•  Labyrinthine concussion
•  Recurrent vestibulopathy
•  Migraine-associated dizziness, benign paroxysmal vertigo of childhood
•  Labyrinthine ischemia
•  Chemical-induced vestibulotoxicity
Modified in Revision 21, October 11, 2024: 3.3  Added in Revision 21, October 11, 2024: Vestibular rehabilitation is covered effective January 13, 2015 for the treatment of vestibular disorders, including benign paroxysmal positional vertigo.

Deleted in Revision 21, October 11, 2024: 4.2  Vestibular rehabilitation therapy (Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) code S9476) for the treatment of benign paroxysmal positional vertigo is unproven.
