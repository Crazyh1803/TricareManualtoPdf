TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 3
Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: Financial Administration
Revision:  C-108, May 18, 2022
Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: All TRICARE requirements regarding financial administration shall apply to the TMEP contractor unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section or the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract. See Chapter 3Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: for additional instructions regarding administration.
1.0  General
Modified in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: 1.1  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The TMEP contractor shall comply with the requirements in Chapter 3, Section 1.Deleted in Revision 108, May 18, 2022:
Modified in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: 1.2  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The TMEP contractor shall comply with the Financial Administration non-financially underwritten funds requirements in Chapter 3Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: .
2.0  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: Payments To Beneficiaries/Providers
2.1  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The TMEP contractor shall comply with the requirements in Chapter 3, Section 2. Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: Paragraphs 2.0 Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: through 2.2 Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) are superseded as described in paragraphs 2.2 Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: through 2.3.Deleted in Revision 108, May 18, 2022:
2.2  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The TMEP contractor shall follow the contract requirements regarding provider EFT. The contractor may make EFT payments to beneficiaries.
Modified in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: 2.3  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The contractor shall make non-underwritten EFTs within two business days after receiving payment approval from DHA. The contractor may pay rejected EFTs with checks. If payment cannot be accomplished as a check or EFT, the contractor shall submit an adjustment/cancellation TED record within five business days or rejection of an EFT.
3.0  Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: Claim Refund And Collection Procedures
Added in Revision 108, May 18, 2022: The contractor shall comply with the requirements in Chapter 3, Section 3, except for Chapter 3, Section 3, paragraphs 2.2 through 2.2.3.
