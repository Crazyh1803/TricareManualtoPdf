TRICARE Reimbursement Manual 6010.64-M, April 2021
Home Health Care (HHC)
Chapter 12
Addendum D
Home Health Certification And Plan Of Care (POC)
Revision:  
Home health care services provided under this chapter must be provided under a POC established and approved by a physician, and the care must be certified by a physician. Please see the Medicare Benefit Policy Manual, Chapter 7, Sections 30.2 and 30.5 for the documentation requirements for the POC and physician certification. This manual is available here: http://www.cms.gov/Regulations-and-Guidance/Guidance/Manuals/Downloads/bp102c07.pdf.
