Nutritionist

1.0  ISSUE
Nutritionist.
2.0  POLICY
2.1  Added in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: A nutritionist must be licensed by the state in which the care is provided and must be under the supervision of a physician who is overseeing the episode of treatment or the covered program of services.
Modified in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: 2.2  Added in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: Effective May 25, 2011, a nutritionist may provide Diabetes Self-Management Training (DSMT) via an accredited DSMT program when the nutritionist:
Modified in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: 2.2.1  Has received at least a bachelor’s degree from an accredited U.S. college or university;
Modified in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: 2.2.2  Is licensed by the State in which the care is provided; and
Modified in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: 2.2.3  Is under the supervision of a physician who is overseeing the DSMT program.
Modified in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: 2.3  Added in Revision 8, August 28, 2017: Effective December 23, 2017, a nutritionist may provide medical nutritional therapy (also referred to as medical nutritional counseling) required in the administration of the medically necessary foods, services and supplies, and medically necessary vitamins authorized in Chapter 8, Section 7.2, or when medically necessary for other authorized covered services.
