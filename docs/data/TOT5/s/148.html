TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 26
Continued Health Care Benefits Program (CHCBP)
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 26 regarding CHCBP shall do not apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
