TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 27
Prime Service Area (PSA) Reduction Policies And Effective Dates
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Added in Revision 12, August 14, 2024: Chapter 27 Deleted in Revision 12, August 14, 2024: 6 regarding PSA reduction polices and effective dates shall not apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section, the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
- END -
