TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 22
Reserve Component (RC) Health Coverage Plans
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
All TRICARE requirements noted in Chapter 22 regarding RC health coverage plans Health Coverage Plans shall apply to TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section (as indicated below), the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  TRICARE Reserve Select (TRS) and TRICARE Retired Reserve (TRR)
1.1  The provisions of requirements in Chapter 22, Section 1, specific to enrollment and collection of premiums are do not applicable to TMEP apply.
1.2  The TMEP contractor shall process claims processing shall follow in accordance with TRM, Chapter 4, Section 4. The TMEP contractor shall perform customer service functions to the same extent as other TMEP beneficiaries. TMEP claims processing will follow TRM, Chapter 4, Section 4, and customer service functions are to meet the requirements of Section 11.
