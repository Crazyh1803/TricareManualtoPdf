TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 18
Demonstrations and Pilot Projects (Except Value-Based Initiatives)
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
AllThe TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements in Chapter 18 regarding Demonstrations and Pilot Projects shall apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section (as indicated below), the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
1.0  General
The provisions of TMEP contractor shall comply with the requirements in Chapter 18 are applicable to TMEP with the following exceptions:
1.1  The provisions of requirements in Chapter 18, Section 3, paragraphs 8.8.1 through 8.8.3.2, referral and authorizations requirements will be conducted by the regional contractors Department of Defense (DoD) Comprehensive Autism Care Demonstration (ACD) - The regional contractors manage the care for beneficiaries receiving ACD services Extended Care Health Option (ECHO) for Active Duty Family Members (ADFMs) which may include TRICARE and Medicare eligible beneficiaries. The TMEP contractor shall process claims for beneficiaries authorized benefits under the ACD that are within the TMEP scope. The regional contractors shall comply with the requirement for referrals and authorizations in Chapter 18, Section 3, paragraph 8.6. The regional contractor will submit shall share the authorizations for TRICARE/Medicare dual eligible beneficiaries to with the TMEP contractor as established in the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). The TMEP contractor will shall contact the appropriate regional contractor regarding referral and authorizations for claims received with ACD services without an authorization.
1.2  The provisions of Chapter 18, Section 4, paragraphs 15.1, 15.6, and 15.8 are not applicable to TMEP.
1.2  The provisions of requirements in Chapter 18, Section 94, paragraphs 3.0 and 6.0 do not apply to TMEP. All other requirements for this Pilot Project do not apply.
