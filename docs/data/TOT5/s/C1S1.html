Organization Of The Defense Health Agency (DHA)
Revision:  
Chapter 55, Title 10, of the United States Code (USC), provides that the Secretary of Defense and the Secretary of Health and Human Services (HHS) will jointly prescribe regulations for the administration of TRICARE. Department of Defense Directive (DoDD) 5136.13 (The DHA Charter) established DHA as an agency under the policy guidance and direction of the Assistant Secretary of Defense (Health Affairs) (ASD(HA)).
