Modified in Revision 48, March 17, 2026: 2.2  Added in Revision 48, March 17, 2026: Human papillomavirus (HPV) testing detects certain strains of HPV that may cause cervical cancer. HPV testing may be performed as a screening test in conjunction with or separately from a Pap test. Reflex testing refers to a diagnostic HPV test performed after a Pap test indicates Atypical Squamous Cells of Undetermined Significance (ASCUS) or low-grade dysplasia of cervical cells.
