TRICARE Policy Manual 6010.63-M, April 2021
Medicine
Chapter 7
Section 18.5
Chiropractic Manipulative Treatment (CMT)
Issue Date:  December 3, 1997
Authority:  32 CFR 199.4(g)(38)
Copyright:  CPT only © 2006 American Medical Association (or such other date of publication of CPT). All Rights Reserved.
Revision:  
1.0  CPT CODE RANGE
98940 - 98943
2.0  DESCRIPTION
CMT is a form of manual treatment, using a variety of techniques, to influence joint and neurophysiological function.
3.0  POLICY
Manipulation and treatment of the structures of the human body provided by a chiropractor may not be cost-shared.
