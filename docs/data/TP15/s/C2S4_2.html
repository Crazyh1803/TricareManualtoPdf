2.0  DESCRIPTION
Services provided by a physician directing the care of a critically ill neonate/infant.
3.0  POLICY
3.1  Neonatal and pediatric critical care services may be cost-shared.
3.2  Hospital-grade electric breast pumps may be covered for premature infants under certain circumstances (see Chapter 8, Section 2.6).
