2.0  DESCRIPTION
A deliberation with a specialist physician, dentist, or qualified mental health provider requested by the attending physician primarily responsible for the medical care of the patient, with respect to the diagnosis or treatment in any particular case.
3.0  POLICY
3.1  Added in Revision 100, August 3, 2022: TRICARE covers consultations performed by a Added in Revision 100, August 3, 2022: TRICARE-authorized, individual professional provider at the request of the patient’s attending provider.Deleted in Revision 100, August 3, 2022:
3.2  A consulting physician, dentist, or qualified mental health provider may perform a limited examination of a given system or one requiring a complete diagnostic history and examination.
3.3  Added in Revision 100, August 3, 2022: The contractor shall require a written report to the attending physician of the consultant’s findings Added in Revision 100, August 3, 2022: to cover a visit as a consultation.
Modified in Revision 100, August 3, 2022: 3.4  Added in Revision 100, August 3, 2022: TRICARE covers interprofessional Internet consultations conducted via the Internet and telephone (Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes 99446-99449, 99451, and 99452), including a verbal and written report to the patient’s treating/requesting physician or other TRICARE-authorized provider. See Chapter 7, Section 22.1.
4.0  EXCLUSIONS
4.1  TelephoneAdded in Revision 100, August 3, 2022: toll charges are not covered.
4.2  Staff consultations required by the policies of a hospital or other institution.
