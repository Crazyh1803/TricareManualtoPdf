TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Medicare Eligible Program (TMEP)
Chapter 20
Section 9
Records Management
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
AllThe TMEP contractor shall comply with all TRICARE requirements in Chapter 9 regarding records management shall apply to the TMEP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section; the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), TRICARE Reimbursement Manual (TRM), TRICARE Systems Manual (TSM), or TMEP contract.
