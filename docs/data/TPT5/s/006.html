Administration
Chapter 2
Section 1.1
Office Visits
Issue Date:  April 19, 1983
Authority:  32 CFR 199.4(c)(2)(iv)

Revision:  C-33, May 13, 2025
1.0  CPT CODES
90801, 90802, 98012 - 98016, 98966 - 98968, 99201 - 99215, 99441 - 99443
2.0  hcpcs
G2012
3.0  POLICY
Office visits are covered when provided by a TRICARE-authorized individual professional provider for the diagnosis or treatment of a specific illness or condition or set of symptoms.
3.1  Telehealth office visits are covered when provided by a TRICARE-authorized, individual professional provider in accordance with Chapter 7, Section 22.1.
3.2  Effective May 12, 2020, telephonic office visits (Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes 98012-98016, 98966-98968, 99441-99443, and Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) G2012) are covered when provided by a TRICARE-authorized, individual professional provider in accordance with Chapter 7, Section 22.1.
4.0  EXCLUSION
Office visits for the purpose of a routine physical examination, except as outlined under preventive care.
