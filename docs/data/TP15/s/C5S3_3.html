Radiology
Chapter 5
Section 3.3
Hyperthermia
Issue Date:  
Authority:  32 CFR 199.4(b)(2), (c)(2)(x), (c)(3)(viii), and (g)(15)
Revision:  
1.0  POLICY
Hyperthermia is covered for those indications documented by reliable evidence as safe, effective and comparable or superior to standard care (proven).
2.0  EXCLUSION
Whole body hyperthermia in the treatment of cancer is unproven. Hyperthermia for recurrent breast cancer is unproven.
- END -
