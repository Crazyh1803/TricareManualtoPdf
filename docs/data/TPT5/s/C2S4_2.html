1.0  CPT CODES
99363, 99364
2.0  DESCRIPTION
Anticoagulant services are intended to describe the outpatient management of Warfarin therapy, including ordering, review, and interpretation of International Normalized Ratio (INR) testing, communication with patient, and dosage adjustments as appropriate.
3.0  POLICY
Outpatient anticoagulation management (Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes 99363 and 99364) for patients receiving long-term anticoagulant therapy (e.g., Warfarin) in the office or outpatient setting may be considered for cost-sharing.
