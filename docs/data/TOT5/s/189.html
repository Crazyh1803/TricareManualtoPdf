TRICARE Operations Manual 6010.62-M, April 2021
TRICARE Overseas Program (TOP)
Chapter 24
Section 26
27
Value-Based Programs (VBPs), Pilots, And Demonstrations
Revision:  C-12, August 14, 2024
1.0  General
All TRICARE requirements regarding demonstrations shall apply to the TOP unless specifically changed, waived, or superseded by this section; the TRICARE Policy Manual (TPM), Chapter 12; or the TRICARE contract for health care support services outside the 50 United States (US) and the District of Columbia (hereinafter referred to as the “TOP contract”). See Chapter 18 for additional instructions.
2.0  Value-Based Initiatives
Beneficiaries enrolled in the TOP are excluded from all value-based initiatives.
